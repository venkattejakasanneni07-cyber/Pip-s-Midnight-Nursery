Meet Pip

Pip is about the height of a teacup, wears a nightcap made from an old star, and has a soft spot for anything that glows. Pip doesn't sleep much — someone has to keep watch over all the dreams that wander out of children's bedrooms at midnight.

Powers: catching falling dreams, humming them back to life, folding starlight into lanterns.

Pip's Story

i. The night the star arrived

One night, a tiny star slipped through Pip's window and landed in a teacup. It wasn't lost — it was carrying something. Pip had never seen a star with pockets before.

ii. What the star was holding

Inside its pockets were dreams — the small, forgotten kind. The star asked Pip to keep them safe.

iii. The nursery that grew

So Pip built a nursery — not for children, but for dreams. A room with a lantern for every lost dream, and a music box for every happy one.
