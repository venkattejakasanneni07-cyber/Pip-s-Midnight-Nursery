Meet Pip, Junior Dream Engineer

Small enough to hide in a teacup. Big enough to hold a dream.

Hi, I'm Pip! I'm a baby with a little skull face, a star nightcap with a pink bow, and heart pyjamas. I'm a Junior Dream Engineer: I fix, polish and deliver dreams, especially forgotten ones, because every dream is still out there, waiting to be found.

One

A tiny star outside the window

One quiet night, Pip noticed a star no bigger than a button, glowing right outside the nursery window. It blinked, just once, as if to say hello.

Two

The jar of forgotten dreams

The star led Pip to a hidden shelf of glass jars. Each one held a dream someone had forgotten by morning, still glowing, still hoping to be remembered.

Three

A nursery for lost dreams

So Pip built a nursery of lantern light, cloud pillows and music-box lullabies, a cosy place where every forgotten dream could be rediscovered and loved again.
